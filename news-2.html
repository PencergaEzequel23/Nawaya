NAWAYA COFFEE PRICE HIKE: CUSTOMERS PREPARE FOR POSSIBLE CHANGES IN COFFEE PRICES

By: News and Information Desk

Coffee lovers may soon need to adjust their budgets as rising coffee production costs put pressure on cafés and coffee shops across the Philippines. Nawaya Coffee customers may be concerned about possible price increases as the coffee industry faces growing challenges in sourcing beans and maintaining affordable menu prices.

According to recent industry reports, coffee prices in the Philippines could increase as early as December 2026 and remain elevated into the first quarter of 2027. Factors contributing to the expected increases include limited supplies of imported coffee beans, lower local production, higher transportation expenses, and rising operational costs. Source: Philstar.com

These developments could put pressure on coffee businesses, particularly smaller cafés that have fewer resources to absorb additional expenses. For customers, higher operating costs may eventually translate into more expensive drinks.

However, there is currently no verified information confirming Nawaya Coffee's exact price adjustments, affected beverages, or implementation date. Customers are encouraged to check the café's official announcements for accurate and updated pricing information.

Despite the possibility of higher prices across the industry, coffee remains a favorite daily treat for many Filipinos. Whether enjoyed during a busy morning or shared with friends, customers continue to value the experience, quality, and comfort that a cup of coffee brings.

As the industry navigates rising costs, the challenge for cafés like Nawaya Coffee will be to balance affordability with the quality and experience their customers expect.
