October 12, 2026

A NEW LITTLE SPOT, A BIG NEW EXPERIENCE.

The wait is finally over! Something beautiful is brewing, and we’re beyond excited to welcome you to our newest location! After all the preparation, passion, and hard work, the moment we’ve been waiting for is finally here.

Whether you’re stopping by for a quick visit, spending quality time with your favorite people, or simply looking for a new spot to unwind, we can’t wait to make you feel right at home.

This new chapter means so much to us, and we’re thrilled to finally share it with all of you. Every little detail has been prepared with love, and we hope our newest space becomes one of your favorite places to create moments worth remembering.

So, gather your friends, bring your loved ones, and be part of something special as we begin this exciting new journey together!
